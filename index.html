How we work

Proven in a running mill before we sell it.

Step 1

Find the loss you've learned to live with

Mix-ups, weight give-away, contamination, absenteeism. We start on your floor, not with a catalogue.

Step 2

Engineer it around your mill

Retrofit-first. We work around your frames, winders and layout, and phase it so production keeps running.

Step 3

Build and commission in-house

Mechanical, PLC, vision software and ERP link all come from our Coimbatore facility. One team is accountable.

Step 4

Support it for its working life

Remote diagnostics, spares from Coimbatore, and training until your own engineers run it confidently.
